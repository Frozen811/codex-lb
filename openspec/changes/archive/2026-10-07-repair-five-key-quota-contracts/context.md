The issue author explicitly accepts either automatic credit accrual with a defined conversion or a clearly documented display-only override. The existing model uses integer Codex override windows, and the project has no reliable model/tier-specific credit conversion. This change retains the display-only contract and removes misleading enforcement expectations.

Quota limits are operator configuration. A nullable account column preserves zero-configuration behavior and lets independent replicas read the same value. Account selection consumes the persisted snapshot rather than a process-local registry.

For example, an account limited to 50% is unavailable at 50% observed consumption; clearing that limit allows it again. Selecting 30 days on the API-key page uses 30 days for both the usage totals and the trend, while lifetime overview figures remain explicitly labeled as lifetime.
